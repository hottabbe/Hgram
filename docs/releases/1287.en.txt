Hgram 13.0.2 - build 1287
Changes since build 1260

ALBUMS
+ Centred swipeable stack with blurred back cards and a media counter.
+ New transition: outgoing card shrinks and blurs, next card grows and sharpens.
+ Media outside the bubble. Caption, reactions, author, views and time in the lower bubble.
+ Adaptive footer height, corrected reaction alignment and restored comments button.

VOICE TRANSCRIPTION
+ Sherpa processes long recordings in 4-7 second segments, cutting at quiet pauses when possible.
+ Partial text appears as segments finish. Vosk now publishes recognized phrases during processing.

FILTERS AND CHAT
+ Custom filter keywords in settings: edit, save and clear the list.
+ Redesigned keyword editor with a rounded field and a hint.
+ Merged messages use the native bubble drawable.

Release APK: arm64-v8a, without R8.
